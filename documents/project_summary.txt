1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & GOAL
--------------------------
This project simulates an emergency crowd evacuation on a university building
floor (FPT University Delta building, Floor 2) using the Helbing-Farkas-Vicsek
(2000) Social Force Model (SFM) in continuous 2D space.

Each of the 4 emergency exits (E1..E4) is equipped with an independent "Smart
Exit Sign" consisting of:
  - A downward-tilted camera (OmniVision OV5648: HFOV 58.6 deg, VFOV 45.3 deg)
    mounted above the exit door looking out into the corridor.
  - An AI crowd counter (simulated with a 1.0 s update period, +/-10% Gaussian
    count error, and a 3.0 s moving-average filter) measuring floor crowd
    density (people/m^2) within its visible cone (accounting for mounting
    blind zone and wall line-of-sight).
  - A 2-state sign controller:
      * GREEN ("OPEN"): Exit is recommended.
      * RED ("CLOSED"): Exit is congested (density > close_above). Note: in the
        current design, "CLOSED" does NOT physically lock the door; the door
        remains physically passable, and the sign turns RED to warn/divert
        approaching evacuees toward less congested exits.

4. MODULE-BY-MODULE TECHNICAL DETAILS
-------------------------------------
A. `sfm/config.py`
   Defines 9 dataclasses bundled into `Config`:
   - `MapConfig`: `image_path="1.png"`, `meters_per_pixel=0.05`, `wall_threshold=127`, `seal_rects=[]`, `exit_door_width_m=None`.
   - `ExitConfig`: `name`, `room: Rect`, `door: Rect`, `inward: Tuple[int, int]`.
   - `PedestrianConfig`: `mass=80.0` kg, `radius=0.25` m (0.50 m shoulder width), `v0_min=1.5` m/s, `v0_max=2.0` m/s, `tau=0.5` s, `v_max_factor=1.3`.
   - `ForceConfig`: `A=2000.0` N, `B=0.08` m, `A_wall=2000.0` N, `B_wall=0.04` m, `k=1.2e5` kg/s^2, `kappa=2.4e5` kg/(m*s), `cutoff=2.0` m, `skin=0.5` m, `noise_std=0.0` N.
   - `NavConfig`: `cell_size=0.1` m (2x2 px grid), `wall_clearance=0.5` m, `wall_penalty=4.0`, `target_depth=1.5` m, `evac_depth=1.0` m, `cache_dir="cache"`.
   - `SensorConfig`: `radius=8.0` m, `fov_deg=58.6`, `line_of_sight=True`, `period=1.0` s, `count_error=0.10`, `use_min_range=True`, `vfov_deg=45.3`, `mount_height=2.5` m, `tilt_deg=45.0` deg, `metric="density"`, `smooth_window=3.0` s.
   - `GateConfig`: `enabled=True`, `close_above=0.7` people/m^2, `reopen_at_most=0.4` people/m^2, `min_closed_time=10.0` s, `max_closed_time=20.0` s (fail-safe reopen), `min_open_time=5.0` s (anti-flicker), `commit_radius=5.0` m.
   - `SpawnConfig`: `n_agents=300`, `door_close_radius_px=10`, `min_wall_gap=0.1` m, `min_agent_gap=0.05` m, `x_min_m=None`, `x_max_m=None`.
   - `SimConfig`: `dt=0.01` s, `t_max=300.0` s, `frame_period=0.1` s, `seed=0`, `danger_force_N=1000.0` N.

B. `sfm/floorplan.py` (`FloorPlan`)
   - Converts `1.png` into boolean `base_free` (True = walkable). Optionally narrows exit doors to `exit_door_width_m`.
   - Computes Signed Distance Field `sdf` (in metres, >0 on floor, <0 inside walls) using `cv2.distanceTransform` on both floor and wall masks, plus unit gradient normals `(nx, ny)` pointing away from the nearest wall.
   - `wall_distance(pos)` and `wall_normal(pos)` use vectorized bilinear interpolation (`bilinear()`).
   - `detect_rooms(door_close_radius_px)`: Erodes `base_free` with an ellipse of radius `door_close_radius_px` (10 px) to pinch shut 15-16 px doorways, labels connected components, grows them back to walls via Voronoi assignment (`cv2.distanceTransformWithLabels`), removes the corridor component (identified by largest bounding-box extent `CC_STAT_WIDTH * CC_STAT_HEIGHT`), removes the 4 exit stairwell rooms, and re-numbers the remaining 34 spawnable rooms `1..34`.

C. `sfm/navigation.py` (`NavigationField`, `build_navigation`)
   - Coarsens `base_free` into `cell_size = 0.1 m` (`2x2` pixel) blocks. A block is passable only if all pixels inside it are floor (`passable = blocks.all(axis=(1, 3))`, preserving 2-px walls).
   - Adds a wall-proximity penalty `cost = 1.0 + wall_penalty * clip(1 - clearance / wall_clearance, 0, 1)^2` so routes stay away from wall corners.
   - Runs 8-connected multi-source Dijkstra from target cells inside each exit room (`depth >= target_depth` past the inner door edge) and computes a normalized upwind descent vector field `dir` (`(2, Hc, Wc)`).
   - Caches results in `cache/nav_<sha1>.npz` (version tag `"v2"`).

D. `sfm/exit_sign.py` (`GateController`, `ExitSign`)
   - Camera position: `door_center - inward * (half_depth + 0.2)`, facing `-inward` (into the corridor).
   - `_ground_range()`: Uses `mount_height` (2.5 m), `tilt_deg` (45 deg), and `vfov_deg` (45.3 deg) to compute the dead/blind zone right under the sign (`near = h / tan(tilt + vfov/2) = 1.03 m`) and far ground intersection (`far = h / tan(tilt - vfov/2) = 6.08 m`).
   - `_visible_mask()`: Floor pixels within `[min_range, min(radius, max_range)]`, outside the exit room, inside the `fov_deg` (58.6 deg) horizontal cone, with unobstructed ray-marched line of sight (`2 * R_px + 1` samples per ray).
   - `update(t, count)`: Applies multiplicative Gaussian counting noise `N(0, count_error)`, divides by `visible_area_m2` when `metric == "density"`, averages over the last `smooth_window / period` readings, and updates `GateController`:
       * `OPEN -> CLOSED` when `value > close_above` AND open for `>= min_open_time`.
       * `CLOSED -> OPEN` when closed for `>= max_closed_time` (fail-safe) OR (closed for `>= min_closed_time` AND `value <= reopen_at_most`).
   - `evacuated(pos)`: Returns True for agents inside `room_m` at depth `>= evac_depth` (1.0 m) past the inner door edge.

E. `sfm/crowd.py` (`Crowd`)
   - Stores active pedestrian state as `(N, 2)` NumPy arrays (`pos`, `vel`) and 1D arrays (`v0`, `radius`, `mass`, `ids`, `target`, `contact`).
   - `assign_nearest_targets()`: Sets initial `self.target = np.argmin(D, axis=1)` where `D` is the `(N, 4)` matrix of geodesic walking distances to the 4 exits.
   - `update_targets(signs, floor, commit_radius=5.0)`: Dynamic evacuee route choice called every frame (`0.1 s`):
       1. Within `commit_radius` (5.0 m) of the door: agent commits to exiting through that door despite a RED signal.
       2. Outside `commit_radius`: if the nearest exit is GREEN (`not sign.closed`), agent heads to the nearest exit.
       3. Outside `commit_radius` and nearest exit is RED (`sign.closed`): agent checks ray-marched line-of-sight (`_can_see_sign`) to all GREEN exit signs (`sign.camera`) and runs to the nearest visible GREEN door (or keeps its current GREEN target if no GREEN sign is currently in line-of-sight).
   - `_neighbour_pairs()`: Verlet neighbour list for agent pairs within `cutoff + skin` (2.5 m), rebuilt only when an agent moves more than `0.5 * skin`.
   - `compute_forces()`: Vectorized Helbing SFM forces (driving force + pairwise social/body/friction forces + SDF wall social/body/friction forces) and records per-agent physical contact compression force `self.contact` (Newtons).
   - `step()`: Semi-implicit Euler integration (`dt = 0.01 s`) with speed clamping at `v_max_factor * v0`.

F. `sfm/simulation.py` (`Simulation`)
   - Spawns `n_agents` randomly across the 34 detected rooms with wall clearance `radius + min_wall_gap` and pairwise clearance `2 * radius + min_agent_gap`, verifying finite geodesic distance to at least one exit.
   - At each step (`dt = 0.01 s`):
       1. Every `sensor_every` steps (1.0 s): calls `sign.count(crowd.pos)` and `sign.update(t, count)`.
       2. Every `frame_every` steps (0.1 s): calls `crowd.update_targets(signs, floor, cfg.gate.commit_radius)`.
       3. Calls `crowd.step(floor, dt)`.
       4. Removes agents where `sign.evacuated(crowd.pos)` is True and records `evac_time` and `evac_exit`.
       5. Every `frame_every` steps (0.1 s): calls `recorder.record()`.


5. CURRENT STATUS & PHASE 4 TASKS (WHAT NEEDS TO BE DONE NEXT)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1 (base SFM), Phase 2 (realistic camera cone + independent sign state
machine), Phase 3 (accurate Floor 2 map `1.png` at 0.05 m/px scale), and baseline
Phase 4 evacuee rerouting logic (`Crowd.update_targets` with `commit_radius = 5.0 m`
and line-of-sight visible GREEN sign selection) are implemented and verified.

Remaining Phase 4 research & experimental tasks from `README.md`:

Task 1: Realistic Agent Population (`n_agents`)
  - Estimate a realistic number of occupants on a typical class-slot floor of
    the FPT University Delta building (Floors 2, 3, 4), accounting for empty
    classrooms, labs, and varying room sizes.

Task 2: Evacuee Decision & Rerouting Logic (`sfm/crowd.py` / `sfm/simulation.py`)
  - Design and implement realistic human route-choice behavior when an agent's
    target exit sign turns RED (`CLOSED`) while another exit sign is GREEN (`OPEN`).
  - Rather than 100% of agents omniscently switching at once (which is
    unrealistic in a chaotic evacuation), model partial/probabilistic compliance
    based on local perception, for example:
      * Visibility / Notice distance: The agent must be within a certain walking
        or line-of-sight distance of the RED sign to notice it is red/congested.
      * Commitment distance: Agents already very close to the exit door (already
        in the immediate queue) may stay committed rather than turning back
        against the crowd flow.
      * Switching probability / reaction delay / relative distance cost: An
        agent decides with some probability `p` (or after a reaction delay, or
        based on distance to alternative GREEN exits) to switch `self.target[i]`
        to an open exit.

Task 3: Benchmark Scenarios (`scenarios.py`)
  - Once the rerouting logic is implemented, design benchmark scenarios
    (e.g., uniform vs. asymmetric crowd loads, varying population sizes such as
    100 vs. 300-400 agents, best-case vs. worst-case) to quantitatively prove
    that the smart exit sign system reduces evacuation time (`T90`, `T100`) and
    crowd-crush danger (`peak_agents_in_danger`, `danger_person_seconds`)
    compared to the baseline (`--no-gates`).
